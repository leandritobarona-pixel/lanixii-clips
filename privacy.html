Privacy Policy: Lanixii clips

Last updated: November 21, 2026

  1. What we collect. When the owner connects their TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as the username). This is used only to publish videos to that account.
  2. How we use it. The data is used only to upload and publish video content on behalf of the account owner. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties.
  3. Storage. Access tokens are stored securely and only for as long as needed. The account owner can revoke access at any time from their TikTok settings (Security & permissions → Manage app permissions).
  4. Data deletion. To request deletion of any stored data, write to lanixiclips@gmail.com. We will delete it promptly.
  5. Changes. We may update this policy, and the date above will change when we do.
  6. Contact. lanixiclips@gmail.com
